Rules for this sheet:
- Section 1 contains ONLY facts graded [P]: a primary or official document was opened and read in the verification passes (verification_legal.md, verification_finance.md, verification_evidence.md) or in the original notes.
- "OCR" in the description means the MHA document is a scanned image read through OCR. Death ex gratia and housing figures were checked by eye against the page image; other amounts from those letters are OCR readings.
- Status: exists = in force or operating; partial = exists but limited, unused or internally inconsistent; gap = a primary document states or shows absence.
- Financial year (FY) and calendar year (CY) figures are not comparable. Rs 1 crore = Rs 10 million.
- Section 3 (place cases) mixes [P] figures with clearly attributed [A] figures; each figure is labelled. Latitude and longitude are approximate map markers added for plotting; they are NOT taken from the source documents and should be checked before publication.
